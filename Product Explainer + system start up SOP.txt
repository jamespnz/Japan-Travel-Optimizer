## 🗺️ Product Profile: React Rail Optimizer (v4.0)

The **React Rail Optimizer** is a high-density, terminal-inspired geospatial decision support dashboard designed to solve a high-value consumer friction point: calculating absolute financial breakeven points for Japanese rail passes (like the JR Pass) while layering real-time, time-aware crowd visualization telemetry across transit routes.

### Core Product Modules

* **The Breakeven Math Engine:** Evaluates complex multi-city itinerary inputs against localized dynamic transit price models to determine exact cost thresholds.
* **Geospatial Crowd Layer:** Integrates Mapbox GL JS to render interactive heatmaps showing peak transit congestion points across major transit sectors (e.g., Tokyo, Tokaido Shinkansen corridors, Osaka).
* **The Command Terminal UI:** Tailored to an ultra-dense, low-latency layout featuring font-mono typography, an analytical control room feel, and a Navy/Slate/Silver color profile.
